03 / How I work

Cloud that holds up
in real operations.

As a cloud architect and DevOps engineer I help teams with AWS infrastructure, automation and generative AI applications. I don't start by picking a service: first I need to understand the system, the risks and the people who will use and operate it.

Operations left me with a simple question: how will this be managed once someone actually starts using it? That is why I look for the simplest solution that is secure, documented and automated where it makes sense. Besides architecture, I care about Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD and the everyday work of the people who keep systems running.

I started in system administration. Through servers, storage, virtualization and data center operations I got to the cloud. More than 30 years in IT help me tell what matters in a design.

AWS Golden Jacket

I'm a holder of the AWS Golden Jacket, which I earned by passing all AWS certification exams available at the time. The jacket is nice. What matters more to me is what I learned along the way and what I can apply in practice.

I don't only gain knowledge, though. As a subject matter expert in the AWS Certification SME Program I contribute to content for AWS Training and Certification, so I help create what others learn from and what they are tested on.

IT service management is part of the technical side too. I hold the ITIL 4 Managing Professional and ITIL v3 Expert certifications. You can find my professional history on LinkedIn and my certification badges on Credly.

And when I'm not working?

Technology is fun for me at home too. I play with Home Assistant, design things for 3D printing and connect devices so that they save me a bit of work. I'm interested in photovoltaics and electromobility. Sometimes it turns into a useful tool, sometimes I just learn something.

I've taken part in several hackathons, including public ones. You can find some of the projects on my Devpost profile.
